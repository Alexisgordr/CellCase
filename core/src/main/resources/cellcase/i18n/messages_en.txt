profile.title = ICdetector local profile (shadow mode)
profile.intro = ICdetector 3.0 learns on each phone how the serving signal of each cell usually looks (level in dBm and, on LTE/NR, RSRQ quality) and records, for every history row, how far the reading is from usual: deviation = |value − mean| / spread. The largest deviation sets the level: below 2, NONE; below 3, LOW; below 4, MODERATE; from 4, HIGH. When it cannot compare (no or little-known reference, another phone model, another technology…) it abstains (ABSTAIN) and says why.
profile.context = It is context, as in ICdetector: it runs in shadow mode, its thresholds are not calibrated, a deviation is not a probability and HIGH is not an alert. CellCase shows and summarises it, but does not use it for episodes, priorities or the assessment.
